## Learning Preferences

The study process should follow these principles:

1. Follow the roadmap in order.
2. Study one topic at a time.
3. Understand the concept before executing commands.
4. Use real-world examples.
5. Practice through small exercises.
6. Explain concepts in my own words.
7. Complete checkpoints before moving ahead.
8. Document problems and troubleshooting lessons.
9. Avoid jumping to advanced topics prematurely.
